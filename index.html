How AI Is For The Better

There are people who are scared that if we make machines smart they will become our superiors like in a scary movie. But I do not believe that is going to happen. In this essay, i will explain why creating artificial intelligence is beneficial for the world. When we improve AI, it helps doctors, factories, and pretty much everything else we can imagine.

Even though AI has some problems it's still super helpful and a total game changer. It changes how people work. It makes life easier and more fun for lots of people. It also changes a ton of other cool stuff.

AI helps us every day especially with health stuff. It can identify issues on X-rays and MRIs that even good doctors sometimes miss, so you can get treated faster and have a better chance of getting better. AI also helps doctors figure out the right treatment just for you. And robot surgeons can do operations better than people, with smaller cuts. Think about your phone – you can talk to it, and it can understand you. When you're shopping online, shops remember what you bought last time and suggest other items that you might be interested in. Cameras even identify faces! Homes can even possess AI that knows when you would like the lights on and what your favorite temperature is. Your car also utilizes AI to plot the best route to take.

Some others fear AI. They worry that robots will take over jobs and that AI will be utilized for bad things, like spying or creating weapons. And it's difficult to understand how AI decides things, which can be an issue. But despite all of these fears AI can be extremely beneficial. We just need to ensure we use it in the right way. The idea of AI taking over the world is more of a scary tale. AI does have issues, but it can accomplish a lot by revolutionizing businesses, helping individuals, and generating new ideas. Instead of being scared, we need to use AI to offer a greater future.AI is not something to fear it's an opportunity to make things easier for everyone. We need people to have new skills so that they can work with AI and develop rules to make sure AI is being used safely.
